E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard

"Turning e-commerce data into actionable business insights."

Project Overview

This project analyzes an e-commerce dataset to uncover insights into sales performance, customer behavior, and delivery efficiency using an interactive Power BI dashboard.

The dashboard brings key business metrics together to help stakeholders monitor revenue trends, identify high-performing categories and regions, and better understand customer experience.

Business Problem

E-commerce businesses generate large volumes of transactional data, making it challenging to quickly understand sales performance, regional trends, and factors affecting customer satisfaction.

The goal was to transform this data into an interactive dashboard that provides a clear view of business performance and customer experience.

Objectives

Analyze overall sales performance.

Identify top-performing product categories.

Compare revenue across different states.

Analyze delivery performance.

Explore the relationship between delivery time and customer satisfaction.

Dashboard Walkthrough

The dashboard contains two main views designed to provide comprehensive business intelligence:

Executive Overview

Includes Revenue KPIs, Revenue Trend, Top 5 Categories & Top 5 States

Delivery & Customer Experience

Includes Review Score Distribution, Delivery Days vs Review Score & Top 10 States by Average Delivery Days

Key Insights

📈

Health & Beauty generated the highest revenue.

📍

São Paulo (SP) contributed the highest sales.

⭐

Most customers gave 5-star reviews.

⏱️

The average delivery time was 12.5 days.

Recommendations

1

Continue investing in high-performing product categories.

2

Improve delivery efficiency in states with longer delivery times.

3

Monitor customer satisfaction alongside delivery performance.

Dataset

📊

Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset (Olist)

Comprehensive transactional data covering orders, products, reviews, and customer locations.
