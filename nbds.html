NBDS — Neural Billing Decision System

An AI-powered billing decision system designed to automate billing anomaly classification and support data-driven decision-making for national utility operations.

Project Overview

National Water Company faced significant challenges in manually classifying billing anomalies and prioritizing high-risk accounts efficiently. This manual bottleneck led to delays in identifying and resolving billing discrepancies.

The Problem

Traditional billing anomaly detection processes relied heavily on manual inspection, which lacked scalability and consistency when handling large volumes of transactional data.

The Solution

We developed an AI-powered billing decision system that automates account classification and supports institutional decision-making through predictive analytics and comprehensive management dashboards.

How It Works

The end-to-end machine learning and operational workflow follows a structured 7-stage pipeline:

01 — Data Sources

Data Ingestion

  • Billing & Consumption Data
  • Complaint Data
02 — Data Preprocessing

Data Preparation & Cleaning

  • Data Cleaning
  • Handling Missing Values
  • Outlier Detection
  • Pattern Recognition
03 — Feature Engineering

Feature Extraction & Target Definition

Consumption Features:
  • Monthly Total Consumption
  • Consumption Change %
  • Rolling Average (3M)
  • Consumption STD (3M)
Target Variable:
  • Has Complaint OR High Consumption
04 — Failure Prediction Model

Machine Learning Modeling

Algorithms:
  • Logistic Regression (Baseline)
  • Random Forest
Output:
  • Failure Probability
05 — Risk Scoring & Classification

Risk Scoring & Evaluation

  • Predicted Probability = Risk Score
  • Threshold Optimization
  • High / Low Risk Classification
Output:
  • Risk Level (High / Low)
06 — Account Prioritization

Prioritization & Ranking

  • Ranking Based on Risk Score
  • Priority Action List
07 — KPI Monitoring

Management Dashboards & Reporting

  • Percentage of high accounts
  • Average Risk Score
  • Risk Distribution
  • Top High-Risk Accounts Identified
